# Product and Source Material

## Business purpose

Studio Pranavi needs a premium public website and a practical CRM for a 10–20 person custom lighting and glass business. The website is a digital showroom and lead-generation channel. The CRM governs the customer-to-payment lifecycle.

## Brand positioning

Preferred positioning:

> Bespoke architectural lighting, decorative lighting and custom glass solutions, designed from concept through installation.

Existing brand line from the project book:

> We design the essence of light.

Treat the wording as source content but confirm final public use with the business owner.

## Source-material workflow

When Studio Pranavi materials are available in the repository or session:

1. Inventory every PPT, PDF, logo, catalogue, photo and video.
2. Extract only approved business-facing content.
3. Build a content matrix: source file, project/product, category, public status, rights/approval status, alt text, usage and owner.
4. Flag low-resolution, duplicate, watermarked or contact-bearing images.
5. Never fabricate missing copy. Create clearly labelled draft copy for owner review.
6. Do not publish client logos or project names without approval.

Recommended repository area:

```text
brand-source/
  business-profile/
  catalogues/
  projects/
  logos/
  photos/
  videos/
  approvals/
```

Do not expose `brand-source` directly through the public web build.

## CRM scope

Keep the CRM visually stable. It should support:

- Leads, ownership, pipeline and follow-ups
- Customers, contacts and addresses
- Site visits and measurements
- Versioned quotations
- Orders, projects, milestones and production
- Installation scheduling and sign-off
- Invoices, payments and outstanding balances
- Website CMS, media and branding administration
- CSV/XLSX import and export
- Notifications, reporting and audit history
