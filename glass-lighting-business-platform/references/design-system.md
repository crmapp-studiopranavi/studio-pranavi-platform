# Design and Content Standards

## Brand direction

Create a premium handcrafted feel suitable for custom decorative glass and lighting:

- Warm neutral base with restrained charcoal, ivory, amber/brass, and optional deep teal accents.
- Large, authentic project photography as the primary visual language.
- Elegant display typography for headings and a highly readable sans-serif for body text.
- Subtle glass, reflection, and light-inspired details without reducing readability.
- Avoid generic neon gradients, excessive glassmorphism, crowded dashboards, and AI-looking placeholder graphics.

All colors, fonts, logos, and contact details must remain configurable.

## Website UX

- Lead with customer outcomes and real project categories.
- Use concise sections, clear service distinctions, and repeated quote/contact actions.
- Portfolio cards need useful captions, not image-only grids.
- Never represent stock images as completed company projects. Label placeholders until replaced by genuine work.

## CRM UX

- Optimize for daily work: dense enough to scan, but not overwhelming.
- Use consistent status badges and one primary action per view.
- Preserve filters in the URL when practical.
- Provide table views for operations and kanban only where stage movement adds value.
- Use Indian formatting for default currency and dates while keeping localization configurable.

## Accessibility

- Maintain adequate contrast.
- Do not encode status only through color.
- Give icon-only controls accessible names.
- Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, and meaningful alt text.
