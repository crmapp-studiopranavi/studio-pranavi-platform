# Product Requirements

## Users

- Visitor: explores services and portfolio, contacts the business, requests a quote.
- Sales/operations user: manages leads, customers, quotes, orders, projects, follow-ups, and payments.
- Admin: manages users, permissions, business settings, service catalogue, and content.

## Public-site conversion path

1. Visitor discovers a service or portfolio project.
2. Visitor sees materials, design options, process, and a clear next step.
3. Visitor submits a quote request with project details and optional images.
4. System creates a lead with source, consent timestamp, and activity entry.
5. Business receives an in-app indicator; notification channels are optional integrations.

## Essential website content model

- Service: title, slug, summary, details, category, hero image, gallery, FAQ, published flag, sort order.
- Portfolio project: title, slug, category, location label, short description, detailed story, materials, completion date, cover image, gallery, featured/published flags.
- Testimonial: person label, project type, quote, rating only if genuine, published flag.
- Business settings: brand name, phone, email, WhatsApp, address, working hours, social links, default SEO.

## CRM business workflow

- Capture and qualify leads.
- Record communications, next action, owner, source, priority, budget, scope, and attachments.
- Schedule and document site visits and measurements.
- Build versioned quotations with line items.
- Convert approved quotation into order/project without retyping customer and scope data.
- Track design/measurement, production, installation, completion, invoice, and payment.
- Preserve a complete activity timeline for accountability.

## MVP boundary

Include auth, leads, customers, activities, tasks, quotes, orders/projects, basic invoices/payments, portfolio management, services management, and uploads.

Exclude by default: inventory accounting, payroll, GST filing, full double-entry bookkeeping, supplier procurement, manufacturing optimization, native mobile apps, and online payment collection. Add these only after explicit scope confirmation.
